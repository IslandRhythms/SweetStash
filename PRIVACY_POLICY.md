# SweetStash Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** July 8, 2026  
**Last updated:** September 29, 2026

SweetStash ("we," "us," or "our") operates the SweetStash mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, how that information is used, and the choices available to you.

SweetStash is a family-friendly app for logging Halloween candy, houses, costumes, and trick-or-treat routes. **The App does not require an account, and the candy, photos, routes, and profile information you enter are stored on your device—not on our servers.**

If you have questions about this policy, use the support contact listed on the App’s store page.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information you provide (stored on your device)

When you use the App, you may enter or create:

- **Profiles** — a display name and optional avatar image
- **Costumes** — costume names and photos
- **Candy logs** — candy names, quantities, optional photos, and favorites
- **Houses** — names, notes, optional photos, and optional location coordinates
- **Sessions** — trick-or-treat session start/end times, linked costume, and session names
- **Route data** — GPS coordinates and timestamps recorded while a session is active and location access is granted
- **App preferences** — such as light/dark theme and the currently selected profile

This information is saved in a local database (`sweetstash.db`) and in the App’s private storage area on your device. **We do not receive or store this content on our own servers.**

### 1.2 Information from your device (with your permission)

Depending on how you use the App, you may grant access to:

- **Location (while using the App)** — to show your position on the map and record your trick-or-treat route during an active session
- **Camera** — to take photos of candy, houses, or costumes
- **Photo library** — to choose existing photos for candy, houses, or costumes

You can deny or later revoke these permissions in your device settings. Some features will not work without the relevant permission (for example, route tracking without location access).

### 1.3 Diagnostic and technical information (sent to a third party)

To keep the App reliable, we use **Sentry**, a crash-reporting and diagnostics service. When the App encounters certain errors or performance issues, Sentry may collect information such as:

- Device type, operating system version, and App version
- Crash logs, error messages, and stack traces
- General device performance data
Sentry is configured **not** to collect personally identifiable information: the App does not send IP addresses, user identifiers, or screen recordings (session replay) to Sentry. We use this diagnostic information only to investigate bugs, improve stability, and understand how the App is performing.

Learn more: [Sentry Privacy Policy](https://sentry.io/privacy/)

We do **not** intentionally send your locally stored candy logs, house notes, photos, or route history to Sentry as part of normal App use. However, an error message could theoretically include a fragment of text involved in the error (for example, a candy name).

### 1.4 Map-related requests

When you view a map in the App, your device requests map tiles and related map services from **Apple Maps** (on iOS) or **Google Maps** (on Android).

Those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address, approximate map area, and device information needed to deliver map tiles. We do not control their data practices. Please review [Apple’s Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) as applicable.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information as follows:

- **Local App data** — to display your profiles, stash, sessions, history, costumes, and maps inside the App
- **Permissions-based data** — only for the features you choose (for example, recording a route during a session)
- **Diagnostic data** — to identify and fix crashes, monitor stability, and improve the App
- **Map requests** — to render maps when you use map features

We do **not** use your information for advertising, profiling for marketing, or selling data to third parties.

---

## 3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We share limited information only in these situations:

| Recipient | Why | What may be shared |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sentry** | Crash reporting and diagnostics | Technical and diagnostic data as described in Section 1.3 |
| **Map providers** | Displaying maps | IP address, map tile requests, and related technical data |
| **Legal requirements** | Compliance with law | Information if required by valid legal process |

Because your trick-or-treat content stays on your device, it is generally **not** shared with us or other users unless you choose to share it yourself outside the App (for example, by exporting or screenshotting content).

## 4. Data Retention

- **On-device data** remains on your device until you clear App data or uninstall the App.
- **Diagnostic data** retained by Sentry is kept according to Sentry’s retention settings and our configuration. We aim to keep only what is needed for debugging and improvement.

## 5. Children’s Privacy

SweetStash is designed for families and trick-or-treaters, including use by children under parental supervision.

- The App does **not** require children to create an online account.
- Profile names and other content you add are stored **locally on the device**, not on our servers.
- We do **not** knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the App for our own online storage or marketing purposes.

Parents and guardians should supervise children’s use of the App and manage device permissions (especially location, camera, and photos). If you believe a child has provided personal information to us directly in a way not described in this policy, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

If you are in the United States, this approach is intended to be consistent with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) for the features described above.

## 6. Your Choices and Rights

You can:

- **Deny or revoke permissions** — in iOS or Android settings at any time
- **Delete on-device data** — by uninstalling the App (or, on Android, clearing the App’s storage in device settings), which removes all profiles, sessions, photos, and other entries
- **Stop diagnostic collection** — uninstalling the App stops further Sentry reports from that installation; you cannot fully use the App without the diagnostic SDK included in the build

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR (EEA/UK) or CCPA/CPRA (California), including rights to access, delete, or object to certain processing. Because most of your App content is stored locally on your device, you can delete it yourself as described above. For requests related to data held by our service providers (such as Sentry), contact us and we will respond as required by applicable law.

We do not discriminate against you for exercising privacy rights.

## 7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information processed by the App and our vendors. Local data is stored in the App’s sandboxed storage on your device. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Because your main data stays on your device, **you are responsible for securing your device** (passcode, backups, and who has physical access to the device).
